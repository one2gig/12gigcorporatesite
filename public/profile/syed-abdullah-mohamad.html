Professional Profile

Syed Abdullah Mohamad is the Founder and Business Development Lead of 12Gig Sdn. Bhd., a Sabah-founded digital service marketplace connecting independent service providers, known as Giggers, with individuals and organisations seeking their skills.

Born in Tawau and based in Kota Kinabalu, Syed Abdullah leads the strategic and business development of 12Gig. His work covers market positioning, institutional engagement, strategic partnerships, community development and the company's longer-term direction.

He founded 12Gig after recognising a persistent economic gap. Many people possess valuable skills, practical experience and the willingness to work, yet they lack an accessible platform through which they can present their services, establish credibility and reach paying customers.

This gap is particularly visible among freelancers, tradespeople, rural service providers, informal earners, micro-entrepreneurs and emerging solo-preneurs. Although their services have economic value, many remain underserved by existing employment systems and digital marketplaces.

12Gig was created to address this problem.

Entrepreneurial Journey

Syed Abdullah's entrepreneurial journey began long before the establishment of 12Gig.

From 2009 to 2016, he served as the Founder and General Manager of Wealthy Empire Management Sdn. Bhd. The venture gave him direct experience in building a business, developing markets, managing operations and dealing with supply-chain and structural limitations.

The experience taught him that a promising product cannot succeed through ambition alone. A business also needs reliable market access, suitable infrastructure, operating discipline and the capacity to respond when conditions change.

He later documented part of this journey in the book Berdengkur Tersungkur: PKSku, Kejayaan yang Tertunda, published in 2017 under the name Syed Abdullah Al-Idrus.

This earlier chapter doesn't define his present work, but the lessons drawn from it became part of the thinking behind 12Gig. They strengthened his belief that entrepreneurs and independent workers need better access to customers, dependable digital systems and an economic environment that allows their skills to be discovered.

The Origin of 12Gig

The idea for 12Gig emerged from Syed Abdullah's observation that Malaysia's visible platform-based gig economy was largely concentrated around e-hailing and food delivery.

Outside these sectors, many technicians, home-service workers, creative professionals, event workers, rural providers and digital freelancers had no central marketplace designed around the way they work.

At the same time, patterns of employment were changing. More people were generating income through independent assignments, short-term projects and multiple skills rather than depending entirely on one permanent position.

Syed Abdullah saw the need for a marketplace that could recognise this changing economic behaviour.

The result was 12Gig, a Gigger-first service platform through which providers can present their abilities, describe their services and build professional credibility. Customers can then discover, compare and select service providers according to their needs.

Why 12Gig Exists

12Gig is built around a straightforward principle: a person's skills, time and experience have economic value.

Many capable individuals remain economically underrepresented because they lack visibility, customer access or the knowledge required to promote their services online. Others may have completed training but still lack a clear pathway from acquiring a skill to earning income from it.

Syed Abdullah founded 12Gig to help close this gap.

The platform is intended to provide an organised digital marketplace where independent workers can establish a presence, connect with customers and gradually develop themselves as service-based entrepreneurs.

This includes individuals who may not identify with conventional entrepreneurship but already earn, or have the potential to earn, through their own services. Within the 12Gig model, they are recognised as Giggers and emerging solo-preneurs.

Role at 12Gig

As Founder and Business Development Lead, Syed Abdullah is responsible for establishing the company's strategic direction and developing the relationships required for its growth.

His responsibilities include:

  • Defining the company's purpose, positioning and long-term direction
  • Developing the business model and market-entry strategy
  • Identifying the economic and market problems that 12Gig should address
  • Building relationships with government agencies and educational institutions
  • Developing collaborations with community and industry organisations
  • Leading engagement with potential investors and strategic partners
  • Supporting the growth of the Gigger and user communities
  • Representing 12Gig in meetings, presentations and public engagements
  • Guiding commercial development and partnership activities
  • Coordinating the company's expansion from Sabah into the wider Malaysian market

The title Business Development Lead reflects his active responsibility within the company's current stage. It recognises his position as founder while clearly stating the function he presently leads.

He works closely with the Co-Founder and Head of Technology, Mohd Redzuan Hiew bin Mohd Daud, and the Co-Founder and Head of Operations, Elaina Sukaimi. Together, the founders oversee the strategic, technical and operational development of 12Gig.

Business Development With an Economic Purpose

Syed Abdullah approaches 12Gig as a commercial venture established to solve a genuine economic problem.

The platform must generate real transactions and sustainable revenue to succeed. Its wider purpose is to make participation in the service economy more accessible to people whose skills are often overlooked by conventional employment and business systems.

This requires more than registering large numbers of users. It requires active Giggers, genuine customer demand, dependable service delivery and repeated transactions.

Syed Abdullah's business-development role therefore concentrates on connecting the platform with communities, institutions and markets that can contribute to both sides of the marketplace.

Sabah as the Starting Point

12Gig was founded in Sabah because the state reflects both the need for and the potential of a service-based digital economy.

Sabah has a wide geographical area, diverse communities and many skilled individuals whose capabilities remain economically underrepresented. Formal employment and conventional business systems don't always reach these groups effectively.

Syed Abdullah believes Sabah offers the right environment in which to test and establish a more inclusive model of digital economic participation.

The Sabah-first approach gives 12Gig a clear starting point. It allows the company to understand local behaviour, test its assumptions and demonstrate whether the platform can create genuine economic activity.

Its ambition, however, extends beyond Sabah. The longer-term objective is to establish 12Gig as a Malaysian marketplace capable of serving Giggers and customers across different locations and service categories.

Institutional and Community Engagement

Syed Abdullah leads the company's engagement with government agencies, higher-learning institutions, training providers and community organisations.

These relationships are intended to connect skills development with market participation. Training may prepare an individual to provide a service, but it doesn't automatically create customers or income.

12Gig seeks to provide the missing connection between capability and opportunity.

Through these engagements, Syed Abdullah promotes closer coordination between training institutions, community development programmes and digital market access. His work also supports wider recognition of Giggers and solo-preneurs as legitimate participants in Malaysia's changing economy.

Current Focus

Syed Abdullah is currently focused on moving 12Gig beyond platform development and testing towards sustained market activity.

His priorities include increasing active participation among Giggers and users, strengthening customer demand, generating consistent transactions and developing collaborations that can support market entry and growth.

He is also working to secure suitable financial and strategic support while ensuring that the company's growth remains proportionate to its operating capacity.

Alongside his entrepreneurial work, he continues his postgraduate academic engagement in areas connected to digital platforms, solo-preneurship, the gig economy and community-based economic development.

Founder's Perspective

Syed Abdullah's journey has been shaped by ambition, practical experience and setbacks.

His earlier business experience taught him that vision without market access is fragile, while a platform without genuine transactions has little economic value. These lessons influence how he approaches the development of 12Gig today.

He believes people shouldn't be measured solely by whether they hold a permanent job. Their knowledge, abilities, time and experience also carry economic value.

Through 12Gig, Syed Abdullah is working to create a marketplace where that value can be presented, recognised and converted into income.
