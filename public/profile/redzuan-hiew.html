Professional Profile

Mohd Redzuan Hiew bin Mohd Daud is the Co-Founder and Head of Technology of 12Gig Sdn. Bhd. He has been involved in developing 12Gig since Q1 2024, when the venture was still at its early ideation and concept-development stage.

As part of the founding team, Redzuan helped transform the original idea behind 12Gig into a functioning digital service marketplace. His role brings together entrepreneurship, platform development and cybersecurity knowledge, supported by practical experience in building both physical and digital ventures.

At 12Gig, he leads the company's technical direction and oversees the continuing development of the platform. His responsibility is to ensure that its technology supports the company's wider mission of connecting independent service providers, known as Giggers, with individuals and organisations seeking their services.

Professional and Entrepreneurial Journey

Before 12Gig, Redzuan developed practical business experience through Smokin Cheeze, a fast-food venture. Building the business exposed him to the realities of creating a brand, managing day-to-day operations and meeting customer expectations within a competitive consumer market.

His entrepreneurial journey later expanded into digital platform development. He developed Rewarding Progress, a reward-based crowdfunding platform designed to connect projects and initiatives with supporters through a structured reward system. The platform remains operational today.

His experience with Rewarding Progress gave him direct exposure to the process of turning an idea into an operating digital product. It also strengthened his understanding of user participation, online trust, platform management and the role technology can play in supporting community-driven initiatives.

This combination of business and platform-building experience became an important foundation for his subsequent involvement in 12Gig.

Academic Background

Redzuan is a graduate of the University Malaysia of Computer Science and Engineering, commonly known as UNIMY, where he majored in cybersecurity.

His academic foundation informs his approach to platform development, particularly in areas involving user information, system integrity, digital transactions and the responsible management of technology-related risks.

This background also supports his responsibility for ensuring that security considerations remain part of the development and continuing improvement of the 12Gig platform.

The Founding of 12Gig

Redzuan became involved with 12Gig as early as 2024, during its ideation stage. At the time, the founding team was exploring how a digital platform could help ordinary people convert their skills, time and experience into accessible income opportunities.

As Co-Founder, Redzuan helped assess the technical and operational requirements needed to turn that vision into a functioning marketplace. His early involvement allowed the platform's technical direction to develop alongside its business purpose and social objectives.

He played a central role in bridging the gap between the founding idea and the digital system required to deliver it. His contribution helped establish the technical foundation upon which 12Gig continues to grow.

Role at 12Gig

As Head of Technology, Redzuan leads the technical direction and continuing development of the 12Gig platform.

His responsibilities include guiding the platform's technical architecture, managing development priorities and coordinating improvements based on business requirements, user behaviour and feedback gathered during platform testing.

His role covers key areas such as:

  • User and Gigger registration and account management
  • Service listing, discovery and booking functions
  • Communication between customers and service providers
  • Transaction and payment-related processes
  • Platform performance, system integrity and security
  • Technical testing and bug resolution
  • Feature development and continuing system improvement
  • Technical preparation for commercial growth and wider adoption

Redzuan also works closely with the other founders and operational team members to ensure that technical decisions remain aligned with the needs of the people using the platform.

Building Technology for Economic Participation

Redzuan's role in 12Gig is guided by the principle that technology should solve genuine economic and social problems.

For 12Gig, this means creating a platform through which independent service providers, skilled individuals and emerging solo-preneurs can present their services, establish credibility and connect with potential customers.

Many capable individuals face barriers when attempting to participate in the digital economy. They may lack an established online presence, access to commercial networks or the resources needed to market their services independently. The 12Gig platform is being developed to reduce these barriers and make digital economic participation more accessible.

Through his technical leadership, Redzuan supports 12Gig's mission to build a trusted and inclusive service marketplace that begins in Sabah and has the potential to serve communities throughout Malaysia.

Current Focus

Redzuan is currently focused on strengthening the technical foundation of 12Gig and preparing the platform for broader market participation.

His work includes improving system reliability, refining the experience of users and Giggers, responding to findings from platform testing and supporting the introduction of functions needed for the company's commercial growth.

Drawing on his experience in food and beverage entrepreneurship, crowdfunding platform development and cybersecurity, he brings both business awareness and technical judgment to the continuing development of 12Gig.

As the platform moves beyond its early development and testing stages, Redzuan remains responsible for ensuring that its technology is practical, secure and capable of supporting a growing digital service marketplace.

Founder's Perspective

Redzuan's professional journey reflects his ability to build ideas from the ground up. From developing Smokin Cheeze and Rewarding Progress to helping establish 12Gig, his experience has centred on turning business concepts into operating ventures.

As Co-Founder and Head of Technology, he remains committed to ensuring that 12Gig develops into a trusted platform capable of creating genuine income opportunities for the people and communities it serves.
