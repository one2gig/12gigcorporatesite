Professional Profile

Elaina Sukaimi is the Co-Founder and Head of Operations of 12Gig Sdn. Bhd. She has been involved in developing 12Gig since its ideation stage in Q1, 2024, contributing to the formation of its operating model, market positioning and community-oriented approach.

As Head of Operations, Elaina oversees the company's end-to-end operational development. She is responsible for translating the founders' strategic direction into coordinated activities across platform operations, Gigger development, user engagement, marketing, community outreach and institutional collaboration.

Her experience in fashion design, food and beverage entrepreneurship, customer service and brand development gives her a practical understanding of how ideas are turned into products, services and operating businesses.

At 12Gig, she applies this experience to developing the internal processes, market activities and community relationships required to support a growing digital service marketplace.

Academic Background

Elaina is a graduate of Universiti Malaya.

Her university education, combined with her professional and entrepreneurial experience, has contributed to her strengths in planning, communication, creative development and organisational management.

These capabilities now support her leadership role at 12Gig, where she coordinates activities across different operational functions and works closely with the company's founders, technical team, Giggers, users and external partners.

Professional and Entrepreneurial Journey

Elaina began her professional journey in the creative industry as a fashion designer. She established Nina Elaina Designs, through which she developed her career as a Sabah-based designer.

Her work in fashion involved creative planning, product development, client management and the process of transforming an original concept into a finished product. She was also associated with Sabah's wider fashion and cultural community, including involvement connected with Borneo Indigenous Fashion Week.

Her entrepreneurial journey later expanded into the food and beverage industry. Together with her sister, Norleen Sukaimi, Elaina co-founded Lyndora Cakes & Cookies, which later developed its presence as Lyndora Artisan Bakes.

Through Lyndora, she gained direct experience in product development, production coordination, customer management, marketing, brand presentation and daily business operations. Her work as a cake-and-pastry entrepreneur also led to public media appearances where she shared her knowledge, creativity and entrepreneurial experience.

Managing ventures across the creative and food and beverage sectors strengthened her ability to coordinate people, products, customers and operating processes. This experience now forms an important part of her leadership approach at 12Gig.

The Founding of 12Gig

Elaina became involved with 12Gig during its early ideation stage in 2024. At the time, the founding team was developing the concept of a digital marketplace that could help ordinary people convert their skills, time and experience into accessible income opportunities.

As Co-Founder, Elaina contributed to shaping how the platform would operate beyond its technical system. This included considering how Giggers would be recruited and introduced to the platform, how users would understand its services and how 12Gig could encourage participation within local communities.

Her understanding of small-business operations and customer behaviour helped the founding team assess how the platform could remain practical and accessible to people with different levels of business and digital experience.

She also contributed to developing 12Gig's community-oriented identity, ensuring that the platform's operating model remained connected to the practical needs of independent workers, customers and small service providers.

Role at 12Gig

As Head of Operations, Elaina leads the development and coordination of 12Gig's operating activities.

Her responsibilities include:

  • Translating company strategies into operating plans and activities
  • Coordinating day-to-day business and platform operations
  • Developing internal operating processes and working procedures
  • Supporting Gigger recruitment, onboarding and participation
  • Coordinating user engagement and customer-support processes
  • Managing feedback received from users and Giggers
  • Supporting platform testing and coordinating follow-up actions
  • Monitoring service-delivery processes and operating standards
  • Coordinating community programmes and outreach activities
  • Supporting relationships with institutions and strategic partners
  • Aligning marketing activities with operational priorities
  • Coordinating internal assignments and implementation timelines
  • Identifying operating gaps and supporting process improvements

Her position connects the company's leadership, technology, marketing and community functions. She helps ensure that plans developed by the founding team are translated into clear responsibilities, coordinated activities and practical outcomes.

Community and Market Development

Elaina plays an important role in building 12Gig's relationship with its target communities.

She supports efforts to introduce independent workers, skilled individuals, micro-entrepreneurs and emerging solo-preneurs to the platform. This includes helping potential Giggers understand how to present their services, engage with customers and participate responsibly in a digital marketplace.

Her work supports individuals who possess valuable skills but may have limited experience in promoting their services online. Drawing from her own entrepreneurial journey, she understands the practical challenges faced by small service providers when building customer trust, managing service quality and establishing a market presence.

Elaina also supports collaboration with educational institutions, government-linked bodies, community organisations and ecosystem partners with shared interests in entrepreneurship, skills development and income generation.

Marketing and Brand Coordination

Marketing remains an important part of Elaina's operational responsibility.

She helps coordinate how 12Gig communicates its purpose to Giggers, users, communities and potential partners. Her creative and entrepreneurial background supports the development of campaigns and engagement activities that are aligned with the company's operational priorities.

Her role is to ensure that marketing activities produce practical outcomes, including increased platform awareness, Gigger participation, user registration, service activity and community engagement.

This alignment between marketing and operations helps 12Gig maintain consistency between what the company communicates and what the platform can deliver.

Operations With a Human Focus

Elaina's approach to operations is grounded in her experience as a creative professional and entrepreneur.

She understands that a digital platform depends on more than technology. It also requires clear communication, dependable processes, responsive support and a positive experience for the people using it.

This human focus is especially important for a platform serving individuals from different professional, economic and digital backgrounds. Elaina helps ensure that operational decisions remain connected to the actual experiences of Giggers and users.

Her responsibility is to bring together the people, processes and activities behind 12Gig so that the platform can serve its community effectively.

Current Focus

Elaina is currently focused on strengthening the operating foundation of 12Gig as the platform prepares for broader market adoption.

Her priorities include refining operating procedures, supporting Gigger and user growth, coordinating platform testing, improving customer-support processes and strengthening communication between the company's operational and technical teams.

She also supports marketing execution, community engagement and partnership activities aimed at increasing public understanding of 12Gig and encouraging responsible participation in the platform.

As 12Gig continues to develop, Elaina's responsibility is to ensure that its operations can support a growing community while maintaining service quality, accountability and alignment with the company's founding purpose.

Founder's Perspective

Elaina's professional journey spans higher education, fashion design, food and beverage entrepreneurship, community engagement and digital platform development.

Each stage has required creativity, discipline and a close understanding of how people respond to products and services. These experiences have shaped her belief that an idea only creates value when it is supported by organised execution and a genuine understanding of the people it is intended to serve.

As Co-Founder and Head of Operations, Elaina remains committed to developing 12Gig as a practical and trusted platform that helps individuals turn their skills and experience into meaningful income opportunities.
